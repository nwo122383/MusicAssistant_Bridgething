# Music Assistant BridgeThing 0.1.5

This update adds remote Music Assistant access through WebRTC, AI Radio, easier
speaker grouping, faster repeat library browsing, and phone media-volume control.
Your existing Home Assistant and direct Music Assistant connection options remain
available.

## Requirements

- BridgeThing device firmware/libbridgething 0.17.1 or newer.
- A current BridgeThing phone app; Android companion 0.17.0 was used for testing.
- A Music Assistant server. This release was tested with MA Server 2.10.13.
- AI Radio requires MA's AI Radio plugin and saved shows configured on the server.

## What's New

- **WebRTC remote access:** connect with your MA Remote ID without exposing MA
  through a public HTTPS server or running a PC relay. The phone settings page
  connects first, then lets you sign in with your MA username and password.
- **AI Radio:** browse and start saved shows from Library > AI Radio, beside Radio.
  Choose the playback speaker in Players. Switching an already-running show asks
  for confirmation.
- **Speaker groups:** separate Speakers/Groups views, compatible member editing,
  current group membership, and a confirmed Ungroup All recovery action.
- **Faster library tabs:** session caching, Recent/Playlists preloading, smaller
  24-item first pages, preserved pagination, and a refresh button. Cached Playlists
  switching measured about 0.12 seconds on the test Car Thing. First loads and
  artwork still depend on the server, network, and Bluetooth link.
- **Phone volume:** assign a phone player using its smartphone button in Players.
  The dial then controls Android's actual media volume through BridgeThing, and
  the volume bar follows the phone's reported level. Other speakers keep their
  normal MA volume controls.
- **Safer car output selection:** a saved phone output waits for that player to
  reconnect instead of silently redirecting playback to a home speaker.
- **Voice playback:** includes the voice command handling developed since 0.1.3,
  using the selected player and MA library matching. Speech recognition and voice
  availability depend on BridgeThing.

## Fixes And Improvements

- Refreshed phone settings integration, device-context handling, and save guards.
- WebRTC connection cancellation and stale-callback protection during recovery.
- Remote artwork can travel through MA's encrypted WebRTC HTTP-proxy channel.
- Speaker-group member lists scroll correctly on the Car Thing.
- The catalog now hosts the matching phone settings page separately so supported
  BridgeThing companions can download it over the internet instead of Bluetooth.

## Install Or Update

Refresh the existing Music Assistant source in BridgeThing and install 0.1.5.
The source URL and app UUID have not changed. Existing app settings should be
retained during a normal in-place update.

For manual installation, use **music-assistant-bridgething-v0.1.5.zip**. Install
the ZIP as a BridgeThing webapp; it is not a firmware image.

## Connecting And Signing In

Open BridgeThing on your phone, choose Music Assistant, then open its settings.

**Music Assistant WebRTC**

1. Enable Remote Access in MA and copy its Remote ID.
2. With Home Assistant Cloud/Nabu Casa, enable WebRTC Connections in HA Cloud
   settings. BridgeThing's TCP-only phone proxy needs TCP/TLS TURN for this route.
3. Choose Music Assistant WebRTC, enter the Remote ID, and press Connect WebRTC.
4. Enter your **Music Assistant** username/password, then press Sign in and save.
   You do not need to obtain a token manually. An existing MA access token is an
   alternative, not an additional requirement.

This form uses an MA account, not Home Assistant OAuth credentials. The password
is not persisted by our app. The returned token is saved for reconnects.

**Home Assistant / Nabu Casa**

Choose that connection path, enter your remote HA HTTPS URL and a **Home Assistant
long-lived access token**, then save. Do not enter an MA token for this path.
AI Radio/group management through HA ingress need HA Supervisor/admin access and
an enabled Music Assistant add-on ingress panel.

**Direct Music Assistant / BridgeThing companion network**

Use an MA server URL and MA authentication. The companion-network route requires
HTTPS. Direct browser mode requires that the Car Thing browser can actually reach
the URL; a home LAN address is not a remote-car connection.

## Phone Audio: Experimental Helper

WebRTC in the webapp supplies remote control; it does not itself turn the Pixel
into an audio receiver. For phone/car audio, the optional Android helper source
in `phone-player/` can activate the official MA Android app's native Sendspin player
through its standard Android media service. It does not replace BridgeThing.

Native phone-player registration and music playback were verified. Automatic
locked-phone/cold-car startup is **not yet verified** and needs the helper's
one-time Android permissions and device association. The helper is not included
in the webapp ZIP, and this release does not publish a helper APK. See its README
for setup/build details. Do not assume this is already a finished hands-free
car-start solution.

## Privacy And Verification

The public app contains no saved home URL, Remote ID, access token, or paired
device details. Users supply their own connection settings. Tokens are retained
in runtime BridgeThing configuration and Car Thing browser storage; treat device
storage and backups accordingly.

Verification: production builds and 62 automated tests pass. Live WebRTC sign-in,
phone playback, Android volume changes, and cached tab switching were tested on
the connected Car Thing/Pixel. Longer Bluetooth/cellular recovery and fully
automatic phone-helper startup remain areas for further device testing.
