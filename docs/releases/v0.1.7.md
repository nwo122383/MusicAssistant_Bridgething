# Music Assistant BridgeThing 0.1.7

## Group Volume And Individual Speakers

Addresses [issue #1](https://github.com/nwo122383/MusicAssistant_Bridgething/issues/1): the volume wheel now controls the selected playback player's group rather than only one member.

- Each dial step adds or subtracts **2 points from each eligible speaker**. For example, 9 and 24 become 11 and 26. This preserves volume offsets rather than using MA's proportional group scaling. Each speaker is limited independently to 0-100.
- Open **Players** and tap the **sliders icon** beside a group or grouped speaker for its volume panel. Choose **Whole group** or an individual member, then use the dial.
- Individual member control does not ungroup speakers, start playback, or change the selected playback device. Closing the panel restores the normal dial target.
- **Sync to lowest** is a separate, optional action that lowers eligible members to the quietest member's level. It never runs automatically. Subsequent equal-point group changes keep matched levels together unless a member reaches a limit or is changed separately.
- Unavailable, powered-off, and non-volume-controllable speakers are skipped. Nested groups and shared members are handled without duplicate adjustments.
- Group membership and connection changes invalidate stale queued adjustments. Failed multi-speaker writes attempt to restore prior levels and report an error if the operation fails.
- Full player-list polling is reduced while the volume panel is open; smaller volume reads maintain the connection health checks.

Standalone volume and the configured Android media-volume route remain unchanged. Group volume through Home Assistant/Nabu Casa uses the existing MA ingress route, with the same add-on and ingress-access requirements as the group editor.

## Larger Fullscreen Now Playing

- A discreet expand icon at the top-right hides the bottom navigation tabs and enlarges the artwork, text, and playback controls.
- Tap the collapse icon or press the physical **Back** button to restore the tabs without stopping playback.
- On the tested 800 x 480 Car Thing at Extra Large display size, artwork grew from 285 to 374 pixels and the play button from 88 to 96 pixels. The normal layout is unchanged.

## Upgrade And Verification

Refresh the existing source in BridgeThing and install **0.1.7**, or import `music-assistant-bridgething-v0.1.7.zip`. This is a webapp bundle, not firmware. The stable app ID, connection paths, and permissions are unchanged; no new token or PC relay is required for this update.

Requires BridgeThing firmware/libbridgething **0.17.1 or newer**. Setup and sign-in instructions remain in the [README](../../README.md).

- All **113 automated tests** passed before release packaging.
- Live Car Thing checks confirmed equal-point group changes and member-only changes, with original levels restored and playback selection/group membership unchanged.
- Sync to lowest was covered by automated tests and deliberately not activated on the live household group.
- Fullscreen was checked on the device for actual enlargement, on-screen control fit, touch exit, and the physical Back path.
